# QueryForge Launch Offer

## Free Product 1 — QueryForge Research Starter Kit

**Promise:** A practical field guide for turning a research goal into responsible, precise public-information queries.

**Includes:**

- The research-brief canvas.
- Fifteen public-information search patterns.
- A short operator reference for phrases, source boundaries, document types, and exclusions.
- A direct call to action to open QueryForge at `https://queryforge.manus.space` after launch.

## Free Product 2 — NSAI Research-to-Automation Scorecard

**Promise:** A self-assessment that helps a small-business owner identify repetitive research, reporting, and monitoring work that can be automated responsibly.

**Includes:**

- A 15-minute workflow inventory.
- A prioritization matrix for effort, frequency, and business impact.
- A direct call to action to visit `https://nsai.tech` for NSAI automation services and QueryForge for controlled public-information research.

## Conversion Path

1. A visitor finds QueryForge through the NSAI homepage or Gumroad.
2. The free product delivers a practical artifact and links back to QueryForge.
3. QueryForge offers saved decks and exports, while NSAI promotes the automation audit and implementation services.
4. Paid products are introduced only after the free resource has provided clear standalone value.
